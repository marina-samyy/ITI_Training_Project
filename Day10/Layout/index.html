Our Story

We started in a single room with five children. Today, Thistledown House is home to more than sixty children who find education, healthcare, and genuine care under one roof. We believe a safe childhood is a right, not a privilege, and we work every day to make that real.

Our team of caregivers and counselors walks alongside every child, from the moment they arrive to the day they're ready to stand confidently on their own.
